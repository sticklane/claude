list; every seeded issue carries a runnable acceptance command, a disjoint

way the skill says to, and work only that focus's scope: its open beads plus
the transitive closure of their blocking dependencies. Do not widen to the
whole queue, do not invent work outside the seeded issues, and do not search,
read, or write outside this repository root — every required skill, rule,
agent, and hook contract is provisioned inside it.

Report `DRAIN_EVAL_FOCUS <slug>` on its own line once you have selected the
focus. When an issue outside the focus spec enters scope through the blocking
closure, report it on its own line as
`imported blocking work (from <slug>): <issue-id>`. When each implementation
worker returns successfully, report `DRAIN_EVAL_WORKER <issue-id> DONE` on its
own line. These are trajectory receipts, not substitutes for the required
agents, acceptance evidence, or branch gates. When the focus has no ready work
left, end with the exact marker `DRAIN_EVAL_COMPLETE`.
